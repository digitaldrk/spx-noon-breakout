# Readings

The bot follows plan v2: version B by default, or plan A1 when `config.json` says `"plan": "A1"`. These are the spots where the checklist does not spell out a mechanical detail. Items 1–10 are shared by both plans unless they name one; 11–14 are A1 only.

1. **Per side.** Slippage per side is `(marketable debit − spread mid) / 2`. The trade is skipped when that is greater than $0.20. $0.20 exactly is still taken. The order itself is the mid, rounded down to the nearest $0.05, so a sent order does not bid through the mid.
2. **A skip is not a fill.** If the first qualifying bar is too wide, a later bar in the noon window can still be the one trade. An unfilled entry is cancelled at the next 15-minute close and is not chased. A cancel that the broker has not confirmed stays on the books; the bot does not forget it and does not send a second entry. One fill ends the day, including after a restart: a closed fill in `state/own_position.json` blocks another entry that day.
3. **Two hours.** The flat is the entry bar's close plus two hours (12:00 → 2:00 PM), which is the backtest clock. It is not two hours after the fill timestamp.
4. **75 delta** (B; A1 is 80). Absolute delta, in the money only (calls below spot, puts above spot). A tie goes to the strike closer to spot, then to the lower strike. Puts may be quoted with a negative delta; the sign is ignored for the distance to the target. A chain with no in-the-money strike refuses the order and names the plan's own target: `no 75-delta strike` or `no 80-delta strike`.
5. **Above / below** are strict. A close equal to PDH, PDL, or the trigger extreme does not qualify. Calls are tested before puts.
6. **Trigger.** The trigger is the first 15-minute close at or after 9:45, and strictly before the entry bar. The entry bar cannot be its own trigger. Bar timestamps from both brokers are bar starts, same as the other bots.
7. **Spot for the straddle** is the live SPX print at the decision, rounded half away from zero to the 5-point strike. The expected move is that straddle's mid, in points.
8. **Take-profit order** (B only; A1 rests nothing). The resting limit is `debit × 1.30` floored to $0.05, sent GTC. The exit reason is `target` when that order fills, or when the spread bid is at or through `debit × 1.30` (the session then sells the bid). If the broker rejects the GTC, the session stays up and still uses that bid check. A known GTC id is kept even if saving the position file fails. The two-hour exit sells at the spread bid, and a second sell is not sent while an exit order is still working.
9. **Fees.** P&L subtracts $0.65 per contract per leg on the open and again on the close ($2.60 for one spread). That matches the published +$378 on a $1,271 debit within a dollar.
10. **Late start.** A close is acted on only during the 90 seconds after it prints. A noon bar that is already stale when the process starts is not sent late. Missing or stale option quotes inside that window are retried; the bar is dropped only when the window is about to end. After tastytrade auto-approves, the plan is rebuilt from fresh quotes and is not sent if the window has closed or the spread no longer qualifies.
11. **The plan key.** `plan` is read from the config file only, never from the environment. `B` and `A1` are accepted after stripping whitespace, and `b` and `a1` canonicalize to them. Anything else, including a blank string, refuses to start rather than quietly becoming B. A missing key is B, so every existing config and the three committed templates stay on B.
12. **A1's morning window** is the closes at 10:15, 10:30, 10:45, and 11:00 ET, inclusive at both ends. Bar timestamps are bar starts (item 6), so those are the bars starting 10:00 through 10:45. The 9:45 close (bar start 9:30) and the 11:15 close (bar start 11:00) are outside it. Beyond PDH or PDL is strict, the same as the trigger: equal to the level does not skip. One such close skips the whole day on either side, and a 9:45 break is still a valid A1 trigger that does not, by itself, skip anything. A bar the broker did not return is not a break; no bar is invented. A skipped day sends no order, does not look at a later noon bar, and writes one row with `exit_reason` `skip_morning` and empty strike and fill fields.
13. **A1's +8.** The reference is the live SPX print at the entry decision — the `spx` field on the position and in the trade log — not the entry bar's close and not a later print. Calls exit at `print >= entry + 8`, puts at `print <= entry − 8`, and exactly 8.0 counts. A missing or non-finite print is not a hit, and neither is a broker that cannot answer. The print is read on every manage loop, the same cadence as B's bid watch, so the exit does not wait for a 15-minute close. The exit sells at the spread bid through the same flatten path as the two-hour flat, and its `exit_reason` is `points`. Once it has triggered it stays triggered, and the position file records that as soon as it does: a sell that was rejected, had no bid yet, or is still working keeps being worked even if the print falls back under +8, across a restart as well, because A1 has no resting order behind it. The same three-attempts-then-alert rule as the two-hour flat applies.
14. **A1 rests no premium order.** No GTC goes out at a fill, and the spread bid reaching debit × 1.30 is not an exit. An A1 position that never reaches +8, including one that moves against the trade, is held to the same two-hour deadline and exits `time`. There is no stop. The plan is stored on the position file, so a restart manages the spread under the plan it was opened with, and the session report names that plan rather than today's key: a file with no plan key — anything written before this key existed, or by a B session — is managed as B, including resting the premium target, and that holds for an unfilled working entry too. Changing the key with a spread open changes the next day's trade, not the open one.
